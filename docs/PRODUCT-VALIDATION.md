# Product validation criteria (Master Spec §46)

Private alpha learning goals (qualitative — not CI-automated).

## Supply

- Worth-selling capabilities exist among OpenClaw users?
- Differentiation from prompts alone (data/tools)?
- Sellers keep machines online?
- Setup simple enough?
- Security model acceptable?

## Demand

- Willingness to pay vs DIY?
- Repeat categories?
- Price vs access/reliability?

## Economics

- Seller margin after provider costs?
- Platform fee sustainable?
- Refund/chargeback rate acceptable?

Track answers in product interviews; link technical readiness to `docs/MVP-SUCCESS-CHECKLIST.md`.
